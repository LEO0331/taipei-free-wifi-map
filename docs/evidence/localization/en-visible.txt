Assumptions, missing evidence and responsible use
Model assumptions
Published Taipei county/district fields establish source administrative labels only; they do not establish polygon membership.
Tier precedence: A primary identity/version/coordinate verification, then B documentation, then C monitoring.
Investigation slots are not equipment allocation approvals.
Known limitations
No RF coverage, uptime, speed, demand or digital exclusion is measured.
Acquisition time for the legacy source is unknown; filesystem timestamps are not retrieval evidence.
Source edition changes are not proof of installations or removals.
All rows are retained; broader scope admits records for investigation without certifying their coordinates.
